How consent is handled

A contact is marked opted in only when the opted_in column holds an explicit affirmative value: true, yes, y, 1, consented or subscribed.

Every other value — including a blank cell or a missing column — imports the contact opted out. They are stored, but excluded from every campaign until consent is recorded through a documented channel. An import never revokes consent already on record.
